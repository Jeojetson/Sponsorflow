# Purdue funding research used in SponsorFlow v5

Validated **July 29, 2026** from official Purdue, Purdue Engineering, BoilerLink/Campus Labs, Purdue Student Government-related, and Purdue for Life pages.

## Important scope note

Public information is significantly more complete for West Lafayette than Indianapolis. SponsorFlow therefore distinguishes:

- **Official current** — current dates or details are publicly posted.
- **Official, deadline not posted** — the program is official, but the next cycle date was not available.
- **Verify current** — Purdue-published details exist, but the current program/application could not be confirmed.
- **Contact required** — a Purdue office must confirm eligibility, amount, or onboarding.

The seeded dates marked as internal targets are planning deadlines, not claims about an official application deadline.

## Context used carefully

A Purdue Engineering report from fall 2023 said PEPC distributed **$12,600 across 21 engineering organizations**, with requests from **$300 to $8,700**. SponsorFlow presents that only as historical context, not a current cap or expected award: https://engineering.purdue.edu/Engr/AboutUs/News/Spotlights/2023/2023-1206-pepc-funding

A Purdue Day of Giving campaign page existed for the **Autonomous RC Club at Purdue Indy** in 2026, supporting the statement that Indianapolis student teams can participate in at least some Purdue for Life fundraising campaigns: https://dayofgiving.purdue.edu/campaigns/autonomous-rc-club-at-purdue-indy-arc

## Financial control reminder

Student-organization contracts, purchasing, deposits, and disbursements must follow the applicable Purdue financial process. West Lafayette's BOSO page states that recognized organizations handle finances through BOSO and should consult BOSO before soliciting services or entering contracts. Indianapolis organizations should ask Student & Community Engagement which current office and process applies to them.
