# Validated sponsor opportunity catalog

Validated on **July 27, 2026** against official company program, education, sponsorship, or community-application pages.

“Validated” means an official public outreach route was confirmed. It does **not** mean that support is guaranteed, that an email inbox has been tested for delivery, or that Purdue Indianapolis ASME automatically meets every eligibility rule. Review the official source before each submission.

## Recommended first wave

For the EV-Kart team, the strongest initial opportunities are the programs with explicit support for collegiate engineering, vehicle, electronics, or manufacturing teams: **SendCutSend, SOLIDWORKS, Ansys, Altair, Altium, PCBWay, Polymaker, igus, Kvaser, and Würth Elektronik**.

## Imported opportunities

### 1. SOLIDWORKS

- **Category:** CAD and simulation software
- **Official route:** FORM — https://www.solidworks.com/product/students/solidworks-sponsorship-student-teams
- **Suggested ask:** complimentary SOLIDWORKS and 3DEXPERIENCE tools, simulation access, training, and student-team support
- **Eligibility/constraint:** Competitive student engineering teams; select the closest collegiate vehicle or SAE pathway on the official page.
- **Personalization angle:** Connect the request to CAD collaboration, structural validation, electrical packaging, and manufacturing drawings for the EV-Kart.
- **Recommended template:** TPL-PROGRAM
- **Official source:** https://www.solidworks.com/product/students/solidworks-sponsorship-student-teams

### 2. Ansys

### 6. SendCutSend

- **Category:** Sheet metal and fabrication
- **Official route:** FORM — https://sendcutsend.com/stem-sponsorships/
- **Suggested ask:** $750–$1,500 in discounted or sponsored laser-cut sheet metal and fabrication services for kart brackets, enclosures, and fixtures
- **Eligibility/constraint:** U.S. or Canadian college engineering teams. The official program has defined application windows and says it typically sponsors 10–15% of applicants.
- **Personalization angle:** Include drawings or renderings for specific brackets, battery enclosures, tabs, guards, or test fixtures and explain why their manufacturing process is a strong fit.
- **Recommended template:** TPL-PROGRAM
- **Official source:** https://sendcutsend.com/stem-sponsorships/

### 7. PCBWay

- **Category:** PCB fabrication and assembly
- **Official route:** EMAIL — sponsor@PCBWay.com
- **Suggested ask:** sponsored or discounted PCB fabrication for battery monitoring, telemetry, controls, or power-distribution boards
- **Eligibility/constraint:** University projects and competition PCBs are explicitly supported; shipping may remain the team’s responsibility.
