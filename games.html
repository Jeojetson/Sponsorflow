How these numbers work

Stats use synced daily results from the timed scoring season. Practice and older scoring seasons are excluded. Each name gets one ranked attempt per game per day.

A day win means the most total points across all six games on a completed Indianapolis calendar day. Positive-score ties share the win. Today’s lead is provisional, and late score syncs can change past winners.

Solve times include only solved puzzles with a recorded timer. The timer starts when you open a puzzle and includes breaks. Accuracy averages all ranked attempts; failed rounds count as 0%. Streaks count consecutive days with any ranked play and stay active through yesterday.
