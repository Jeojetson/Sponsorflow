Purdue ASME brings students together through hands-on projects, workshops, industry connections, and social events. There is room here for anyone interested in engineering—not just one major or one level of experience.

Our EV-Kart program connects mechanical design, wiring, battery development, software, and manufacturing. Members work together from the first drawing to assembly, testing, and competition.
